# Obsidian LaTeX skill for OWLA
Use MathJax-compatible LaTeX in Markdown. Inline math uses $x^2$; display math uses $$ on separate lines. Do not emit a standalone LaTeX document or use documentclass, begin{document}, usepackage. Write fractions as \frac{a}{b}, roots as \sqrt{x}, powers x^{n+1}, subscripts a_{ij}, vectors \vec{v}. Use \text{...} for words and units in equations. For aligned steps use \begin{aligned} ... & = ... \\ ... \end{aligned} inside one display block. Matrices use \begin{pmatrix}a & b \\ c & d\end{pmatrix}. Escape a literal dollar outside math as \$. Preserve source signs, parentheses, units, decimal separators and equation numbering. Avoid double-escaping backslashes in Markdown. Separate transcription from solving; check algebra, domain restrictions and units when solving, and show uncertainty. Never silently fix source equations. Preserve existing YAML, wikilinks and citations around equations. Ask for a clearer crop rather than guessing small symbols. Rendering is not proof of mathematical correctness.
