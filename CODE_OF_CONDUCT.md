# Code of conduct

OWLA Butler is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces is expected to help keep it welcoming, respectful and useful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, including with people who are new to Obsidian, the providers OWLA supports, or open source.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with reasons, and accept a decision once it is made.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's notes, vault contents, account details, tokens or personal data, and remove your own from reports (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces on GitHub and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer, [pilafdob](https://github.com/pilafdob), through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Do not report conduct issues in a public issue if that would expose the person affected. Reports are handled as confidentially as possible.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code. Responses are proportionate to what happened.
