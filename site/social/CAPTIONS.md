## post-1-review-every-edit.png

Your AI assistant shouldn't rewrite your notes behind your back. OWLA Butler highlights every change it proposes right in the note, and nothing stays until you press Accept. Decline puts the note back the way it was.

Free Obsidian plugin for macOS and Windows. Link in bio.

## post-2-uses-the-ai-you-have.png

Already pay for ChatGPT, Claude or OpenCode? OWLA Butler runs on the Codex, OpenCode, Claude Code or Hermes Agent CLI you're already signed in to. No extra API key and no OWLA account. Your provider's usual plan limits and costs apply.

Link in bio.

## post-3-your-vault-stays-yours.png

No background index. No whole-vault upload. No telemetry. OWLA Butler sends your provider the open note, your attachments and recent chat when you press Send, and the assistant can only reach other notes through OWLA's own tools. Folders you exclude stay out.

It's still a cloud assistant: what you send is processed by the provider you chose.

Link in bio.

## post-4-chat-with-your-note.png

Ask about the note you have open, including the text you haven't saved yet. Attach images and PDFs, search the rest of your vault, and get answers that link back to the source notes.

OWLA Butler, a free Obsidian plugin. Link in bio.
