# OWLA Butler 3.0.1

- The **Automatic** provider no longer reaches for the most expensive model on easy work. Describing or summarising a PDF, writing a talk script or essay from material you supply, and everyday edits now go to an efficient model at low or medium effort; frontier models and maximum effort are kept for rigorous proofs, deep research and hard analysis.
- Automatic selection now weighs your remaining usage more strongly: a plan that is half used makes its expensive models about twice as costly, so OWLA saves the rest for work that needs it.
- Requests that read attachments or change your note are always treated as needing OWLA's vault tools, so the choice favours models that handle edits reliably.
- Automatic selection retries once if Apple’s on-device model is slow or returns an unclear answer, and understands more ways of describing a task.

Update through Obsidian Community plugins or replace `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` together. Keep `data.json` when updating manually.
