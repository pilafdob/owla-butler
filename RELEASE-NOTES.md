# OWLA Butler 1.5.0

- Ask OWLA to move or rename files and folders, including in bulk. Review the old and new paths, folders to create, and notes whose links may change before accepting. Turn on Obsidian’s **Automatically update internal links** to apply a batch.
- Codex warms up as you type. An unused warm session shuts down after 20 seconds, and the status dot shows whether OWLA’s Codex process is ready. Your conversation stays open between replies.
- The sidebar and tab are labelled **OWLA**. The reply label animates while working, activity is collapsed by default, and Copy Markdown sits beside Sources & activity.
- Starting a new chat asks for confirmation when there is an existing conversation or draft. You can save recent messages before starting over.

Update through Obsidian Community plugins or replace `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` together. Keep `data.json` when updating manually.
